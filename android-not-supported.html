The Science of Sound

By Rio Miyakawa Baptista

Sound is everywhere, wherever we go. We use sound as a form of communication and entertainment as well as an aid to a balance and direction. We can also use sound sound for other tasks such as mapping, medical uses, and pest control. I chose this project because I wanted to explore more about how you can interact with sound and the fundamentals of this "sound" that we use everyday. its source. Or you can imagine sound as an oscillation of matter, slowly propagating through a medium.
